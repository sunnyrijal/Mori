# Context Document for LLM: Gozi (AI Companion)

## Overview
Gozi (formerly DailyPal) is a proactive, AI-driven companion application. It is **not** a habit tracker. Instead, Gozi is designed to help people make the most of their day by providing hyper-personalized suggestions based on what they already have (their inventory) and what is happening around them (local activities, parks, arts, festivals). Gozi uses voice/chat interfaces and generative AI (Gemini) to actively suggest "what to do right now" to maximize downtime and overcome choice paralysis.

## Key Features & Capabilities
1. **AI Chat & Voice Sessions:** Conversational interface for users to interact with Gozi, share their mood, and ask for immediate suggestions on what to do.
2. **Context-Aware Local Suggestions:** Recommends "what to do" with a strong focus on nearby activities (e.g., walking in a local park, checking out nearby arts, current festivals, or hitting up a local cafe) based on:
   - **Current Mood:** Energized, Focused, Tired, Anxious, etc.
   - **Social Context:** Just Me, My Partner, With Friends.
   - **Time Availability:** < 30 mins, 1-2 hours, Half-day, Full-day.
3. **Inventory-Based Ideas:** Users log what they already have at home (food ingredients, hobby supplies, games), and Gozi suggests creative ways to use them—combating decision fatigue and avoiding unnecessary spending.
4. **Behavioral Gamification:** Users level up, earn XP and Gold, and unlock achievements simply by completing suggested activities, hitting aspirations, and engaging with their local environment.
5. **Relationship & Social Sync:** 'Shared Context' allows users to link with partners or friends to find mutual activities, coordinate schedules, and discover local spots together.
6. **Proactive Check-Ins & Weekly Reflections:** Daily Wellness/Refresh check-ins and structured end-of-week surveys that refine the AI's understanding of the user's tastes and lifestyle.

## Target Audience (Hypothesis)
- **Spontaneous Explorers:** People who want to discover local hidden gems, festivals, and activities without spending hours researching.
- **Busy Professionals / Students:** People facing decision fatigue who need quick, curated suggestions for how to spend their limited downtime efficiently.
- **Couples & Friend Groups:** Users wanting to effortlessly coordinate "Date Nights" or group outings in their city.
- **Resourceful Individuals:** Users who want to make the most of what they already own (hobbies, pantry items) rather than constantly buying new things.

## Monetization & Tech Stack
- **Monetization:** Freemium model with a 'Premium' subscription tier powered by Stripe & RevenueCat.
- **Tech Stack:** React, Node.js, Express, Vite, Drizzle ORM, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Google Gemini API, Capacitor for mobile deployment.
